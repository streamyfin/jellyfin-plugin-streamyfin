Home

The rows of the app's home screen, for everyone, in the order they appear. Once saved, they replace the app's own home screen: its Continue watching, Next up and library rows are drawn only while nothing is set here.

Each section is one row that scrolls sideways, filled by one source: items you choose, the next episodes, what was added lately, or an address of the server, such as the plugin's My media and For you rows. Its shape picks the cards: posters, or wide 16:9 pictures. Drag a section by its handle to move it, or use its arrows.

A group, or one person, can have a home screen of their own that wins over this one. The dashboard cannot build those yet; the API and a backup file can. Headers and query parameters for an address are written in the Yaml tab.
